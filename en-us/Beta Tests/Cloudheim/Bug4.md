# 🎮 Clouheim - Beta

📅 Beta Dates: 07/18/2025 - 07/21/2025

🖥️ Platform: PC

📝 Description: Accessing areas outside the map

⚠ Bug found - 🔸 Exploit / Design and Visual – Allows access to unintended areas and reveals visual flaws

After exiting what appears to be a prison, go left, climb onto the branch or root of a tree, and follow along the edge. Then, jump to a higher section on the 
right and turn toward the tree on the left. If you fall, you’ll once again end up in the “limbo.” Falling there teleports you back to the beginning of the stage.
