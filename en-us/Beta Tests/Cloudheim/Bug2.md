# 🎮 Clouheim - Beta

📅 Beta Dates: 07/18/2025 - 07/21/2025

🖥️ Platform: PC

📝 Description: Floating tree visible in the background

⚠ Bug found - 🔹 Visual / Aesthetic – The issue does not affect gameplay but impacts visual immersion.

During the second tutorial area, by climbing one of the stone pillars located to the right of the gate, it’s possible to see a floating tree in the background. 
This happens when using the triple jump (a character mechanic) at the top of the pillar, reaching a higher viewing angle.
