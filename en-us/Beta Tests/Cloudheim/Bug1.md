# 🎮 Clouheim - Beta

📅 Beta Dates: 07/18/2025 - 07/21/2025

🖥️ Platform: PC

📝 Description: Accessing areas outside the map

⚠ Bug found - 🔸 Exploit / Design and Visual – Allows access to unintended areas and reveals visual flaws

Right at the beginning of the game, near the statue of a turtle with people on its back, the player can climb onto the turtle’s shell and, by using the three available jumps, reach the stone edges on the sides of the area. This grants access to an unintended region of the map — an area with no collision or defined boundaries (“limbo”).

When falling into this space, the character is automatically teleported to the next stage of the tutorial, skipping a small mandatory puzzle located just beyond the statue.
