## 🎮 General Analysis – Fading Echo (Alpha Test)

- **1. General Information**

Version tested: Alpha
Testing period: 09/29/2025 – 10/19/2025
Platform: PC
Total gameplay duration: 4 hours

- **2. Initial Impressions**

  My initial impression of the game wasn’t very positive, since on the very first launch it crashed and showed a “Fatal Error” message. However, it opened normally on subsequent attempts without any similar issues.

  <img width="225" height="139" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/52968b2e-f082-4a08-a0e5-067d0e41ff38" />

  Regarding visuals and audio, the overall impression is quite positive. Each map has an impressive atmosphere. Since this is an Alpha version, I was expecting to find bugged textures or even missing ones, as seen in the last available map at the end of the game. Still, I managed to find and access several places meant only for background detail — areas made of walls or rocks without physics or collision.

  As for sound effects, nothing particularly stood out, but the music and sound design are good overall, and the character volume balance is well done. The only minor issue is that the “Glitches” sound effect is a bit too loud; however, since players don’t stay near them for long, it’s not a major inconvenience.

  Regarding the tutorial, I’d say it’s well executed — it presents only the basic information, allowing players to learn the rest by themselves or through community interaction.

  The menu interface on PC sometimes stops recognizing the mouse (I couldn’t identify the cause of this bug). Another issue I would consider a visual bug happens in the graphics settings menu: even when selecting a different graphics quality, after exiting and returning, the setting always appears as “Low.”

  Another negative point is that the “Invert X Axis” and “Invert Y Axis” buttons and the graphics menu lack clear visual indicators to show whether they are enabled or disabled.
  Even when playing on “Epic” settings, reopening the menu always shows “Low” as the selected option.

  With my configuration (Ryzen 7 2700, Nvidia GTX 1660 Super, 16GB RAM), FPS remains between 30 and 50 most of the time, even on “Epic” quality, though a brief FPS drop may occur when moving the camera quickly.

- **3. Level Design**

  I found the maps to be rather short — I’m not sure if that’s due to the Alpha stage or if there are plans to expand them in the future to make exploration more engaging.

  Speaking of exploration, the game does feature some chests, but they aren’t very well hidden, and the current reward (a crystal that significantly increases the XP bar to unlock new abilities) feels somewhat underwhelming.

  In addition, there are several areas that can be “invaded” by using the water forms — especially the green one. You can exploit its jumping ability to reach unintended zones with intangible walls.

- **4. Interface and User Experience**

  The dialogue windows in “Fading Echo” mode feel a bit uncomfortable due to the amount of space they take up on screen. The “Classic” mode is more pleasant, as it doesn’t obstruct the view and allows more appreciation of the game’s design.
  <img width="1920" height="1080" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a3468c6c-244f-418d-8a08-84fd1793e5df" />

  The subtitles appear to be correct and well-synchronized with the audio. I would only suggest considering translations and voice-overs into other languages, such as Portuguese, if possible.

- **5. Narrative and Atmosphere**

  Even though the cutscenes are still under development, the rough sketches already give a sense of how the story is taking shape. Due to the short testing time, I wasn’t able to form a strong connection with the main character, as only a small portion of the story has been revealed so far.

- **6. General Conclusion**

  Overall, the experience with Fading Echo – Alpha was positive and shows a project with strong potential. Even with the presence of some technical errors and visual bugs — expected for a work-in-progress version — it’s clear that a solid foundation is being built, both in artistic direction and in core mechanics.

  The visuals are one of the game’s strongest aspects, with well-crafted environments and an atmosphere that enhances immersion. Performance remains stable most of the time, even at higher graphic settings, which is a good indication of optimization. Minor issues such as collision gaps, menu inconsistencies, and occasional FPS fluctuations don’t significantly impact gameplay.

  The level design, while still limited in scope, demonstrates good exploration ideas and shows clear potential for growth in future versions. The interface, especially in dialogue and menu sections, could benefit from improved clarity and responsiveness. The narrative, though still in its early stages, provides a solid foundation and sparks curiosity about the protagonist’s development and the game’s world.
